ART DIRECTION
A scientific folio from a ceremonial technology institute. Antique copperplate engraving, anatomical precision, and the quiet authority of an archival laboratory plate. Draw on ceremonial geometry and the architectural solemnity of the Vatican, with the experimental intelligence of Bell Labs. Restrained, mysterious, exact. Avoid fantasy-game imagery, cartoon cobras, generic glowing brains, circuit-board stock art, and literal religious or occult logos. No text, lettering, numbers, signatures, or watermarks; the website supplies all labels.
Palette: ink aubergine #34203F, deep purple #291231, soft violet #E8DFEE, muted purple #83558E, restrained magenta #983E7E, ivory-white linework.

Format: three separate square transparent PNGs, at least 1200 x 1200 pixels each.
Use: optional small engraved figures above the three practice statements.
Description: Make three companion engravings with identical line weight, scale, and ivory-white ink on transparency.
